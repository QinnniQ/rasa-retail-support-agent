# Design reference

Reviewed [Kruidvat's Dutch homepage](https://www.kruidvat.nl/) and [customer-service entry](https://www.kruidvat.nl/service) on 22 October 2026. The public site foregrounds shopping categories, promotions, search, and a visible customer-service route. This portfolio demonstration borrows the broad retail cues of bold red, yellow emphasis, accessible Dutch copy, and task-oriented navigation. Its logo mark, assistant character, layout, illustrations, and content were created for this project. No Kruidvat marketing image or official logo is copied.

The site repeatedly labels itself as an independent portfolio demo with fictional data. This distinction must remain visible in any deployment or screenshot.
