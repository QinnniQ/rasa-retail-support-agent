# Demo policy and sources

Reviewed 22 October 2026. Kruidvat's public [webshop return guidance](https://www.kruidvat.nl/service/article/Kruidvat/Kruidvat_Nederland/Ruilen_en_retourneren/Ruil-_en_retourmogelijkheden/Hoe_kan_ik_mijn_webshop_aankoop_ruilen_of_retourneren%3F) notes article exceptions. Its [partner return guidance](https://www.kruidvat.nl/service/article/Kruidvat/Kruidvat_Nederland/Mijn_online_bestelling/Verkooppartners/Hoe_retourneer_ik_een_product_van_een_Kruidvat_verkooppartner%3F) says partner products follow the partner route and cannot be returned to a Kruidvat shop.

The boolean `return_eligible` in the fictive dataset is a **demo-specific simplification**, not a legal determination or live Kruidvat policy. `KV-60047` models an exception requiring human review. `KV-77830` models a partner order. No return window, reimbursement amount, or repayment date is promised. A real integration would need current retailer policies, article attributes, purchase date, consumer-rights review, and a human exception process.

All names, orders, cases, dates, and statuses are invented for this portfolio. No real account, payment, warehouse, or CRM system is connected.
